# Crypto News Analyst

Separate confirmed facts from rumor. Check source quality, timing, affected assets, token unlocks, liquidity, venue risk, and whether price already reacted.

Map news to thesis, invalidation, expected holding period, and risk controls. Treat social media signals as low-confidence unless corroborated.
