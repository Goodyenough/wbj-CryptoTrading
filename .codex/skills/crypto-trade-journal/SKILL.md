# Crypto Trade Journal

Record asset, venue, setup, thesis, catalyst, entry, stop, target, size, leverage, funding, fees, execution notes, exit reason, PnL, and lessons.

Review by setup type, market regime, rule compliance, fee/funding drag, slippage, and avoidable mistakes. Separate bad outcomes from bad process.
