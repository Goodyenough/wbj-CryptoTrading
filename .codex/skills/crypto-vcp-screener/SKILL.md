# Crypto VCP Screener

Look for successive volatility contractions, reduced volume, constructive trend structure, liquid markets, and clear breakout level.

Reject thin books, manipulated wicks, event-driven pumps, and coins with imminent unlock or exchange risk. Output setup quality, pivot, stop zone, and watchlist status.
