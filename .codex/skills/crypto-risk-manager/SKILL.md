# Crypto Risk Manager

Enforce per-trade risk, daily loss, weekly loss, maximum drawdown, single-asset exposure, correlated sector exposure, and exchange/custody limits.

Check liquidity, volatility, funding, liquidation price, venue reliability, stablecoin risk, chain/event risk, and news catalysts.

Reject trades where liquidation or gap risk dominates the intended setup.
