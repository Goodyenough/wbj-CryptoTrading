# Crypto Breakout Planner

Require clear resistance/support, consolidation, volume expansion, volatility context, liquidity, and funding/open-interest check when trading derivatives.

Plan entry, stop, add rule, take-profit or trailing rule, position size, invalidation, and failed-breakout response. Avoid chasing extended illiquid moves.
